# FinBridge Win 11 Migration – Self-Service Answers

---

## 1. I'm Locked Out of AVD – I Can't Log In

**What you can try:**

1. Wait 15 minutes and try logging in again. After a failed login, your account may be temporarily locked. A short wait often clears this automatically.
2. If you can access another device or phone, go to the password reset portal at `https://passwordreset.microsoft.com`. Follow the steps to unlock your account and reset your password.

**When to contact Service Desk:**

If you're still locked out after 15 minutes, or if the password reset doesn't work, **contact the Service Desk right away** with:
- Your name and employee ID
- The time you first tried to log in
- How many times you've attempted to log in
- Whether you've changed your password recently

---

## 2. My Printer on Floor 3 Isn't Printing – It Won't Map

**What you can try:**

1. Sign out completely and sign back in to your computer. Sometimes the printer connection is set up during login and needs a fresh restart.
2. If that doesn't work, try manually adding the printer. Go to **Settings > Devices > Printers & Scanners > Add a printer**, search for "Floor 3" or ask a colleague for the exact printer name, then add it.

**When to contact Service Desk:**

If the printer still won't map or print after these steps, **contact the Service Desk** with:
- Your computer name (type `hostname` into Command Prompt)
- The exact printer name
- Whether the printer worked before today
- When you last successfully printed to this printer

---

## 3. My OneDrive Files Are Missing – I Can't Find Them

**What you can try:**

1. Don't panic. Check the **Recycle Bin** both in OneDrive (online at `onedrive.microsoft.com`) and on your computer — files may have been moved there during migration.
2. Search by filename: go to `onedrive.microsoft.com`, use the search box at the top, and search for the file name or a key word from it. Files might be in a different folder than expected.

**When to contact Service Desk:**

If you still can't find your files, **contact the Service Desk immediately** with:
- The exact filename or folder name you're looking for
- When you last used this file (before or after migration)
- The file type (Word, Excel, PDF, etc.)
- Whether you shared this file with others
- Your deadline for needing this file
