## What You Can Try Right Now

If you cannot log in to AVD:

1. **Wait 15 minutes** and try signing in again. After a failed login attempt, your account may be temporarily locked. A short wait often clears this automatically.

2. **Reset your password** at `https://passwordreset.microsoft.com` — use your phone or another device if needed. Then try logging back in.

These steps resolve most lockout cases without further help.
