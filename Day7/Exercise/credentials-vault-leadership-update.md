# Leadership Update: Shared Credentials Vault — Executive Summary

---

The recent update has largely landed well for most users, with feedback noting a smoother experience than previous rollouts, improved interface aesthetics, and no issues reported by a portion of the team. However, a significant problem emerged at the same time as the update went live: a team has lost access to the shared credentials vault entirely. This is not a minor inconvenience — without access to the vault, affected staff cannot retrieve the passwords and access details they need to do their jobs. The issue has persisted for three days and has been escalated through the management chain.

This remains open and unresolved as of today. The team impacted is fully blocked, and the situation is being treated as a priority incident. The focus right now is on restoring vault access as quickly as possible, identifying what in the update caused the break, and ensuring the same disruption cannot happen to other teams. A fix is being actively worked, and impacted users will be updated as soon as access is restored. There are no workarounds currently available that fully replace vault access, so resolution — not mitigation — is the goal.
