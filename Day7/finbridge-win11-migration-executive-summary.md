# FinBridge Win 11 Migration – Executive Summary

**For:** Senior Leadership  
**From:** IT Service Desk  
**Date:** 2026-08-12  

---

The Windows 11 migration for FinBridge staff has broadly been a success. The majority of users are working well on the new setup, and week-on-week feedback shows clear improvement — the issues that caused the most disruption in the first week, including staff being locked out of their accounts, files going missing from OneDrive, and the VPN dropping during client calls, have all been resolved. Login times are back to normal, files are accessible, and positive feedback from staff has increased since week one. The new laptops are faster and more reliable, and a meaningful number of staff have specifically praised the migration team for a smooth experience.

Two issues remain open and are being actively managed. First, the shared printer on the third floor has not worked correctly since migration day and is now entering its third week unresolved — the team on that floor has been walking to another floor to print, which is not acceptable for the long term. A fix or hardware replacement is being progressed as a priority. Second, a new problem has emerged this week: staff computers are taking significantly longer to reach a working state each morning, and a number of colleagues in the finance team are experiencing Excel crashes when working on large files. Both issues have been traced to a security settings update applied last week. The investigation is underway to identify which part of that update is causing the slowdown and crashes, with the aim of adjusting the settings to remove the impact without compromising the security controls. Updates on both issues will be provided by end of week.
