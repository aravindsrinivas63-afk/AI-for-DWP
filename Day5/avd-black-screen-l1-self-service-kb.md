# Work Desktop Black Screen: Self-Help Guide
Version: v 1.0
Date: 07/08/2026
Status: Draft

If your work desktop opens to a black screen, do not worry. Your files and company data are safe. This problem affects what you see on screen and does not delete your work.

## What You Can Do

1. Wait 60 seconds after you log in.
Expected result: The desktop loads normally.

2. If the screen is still black, sign out of the work desktop app.
Expected result: You return to the log in screen.

3. Close the app fully.
Expected result: The app is no longer running.

4. Open the app again and log in again.
Expected result: Desktop loads correctly.

5. If the screen is still black, restart your laptop.
Expected result: You can try logging in again.

6. Log in one more time after restart.
Expected result: Desktop opens as normal.

7. If the problem continues, stop trying again and contact the Service Desk.
Expected result: Your support request is sent to the support team for quick help.

## Information to Include in Your Support Request

- Your full name
- Your work username or email
- The exact time the problem happened (include your local time, for example UK time)
- What you saw on screen (for example: black screen after sign-in)
- Whether it recovered after waiting
- The app you used (work desktop app or browser)
- Your device type (laptop or desktop)
- A screenshot or photo of the screen, if possible

Contact the Service Desk if the black screen is still there after one restart and one more log in attempt, or if you are unable to work.
