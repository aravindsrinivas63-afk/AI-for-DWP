# Finance Shared Drive Access - Self-Service Help
Version: v 2.0
Date: 07/08/2026
Status: Draft

Your data is safe. This issue usually means your Finance shared folder did not connect properly when you signed in.

## What You May Notice
- The Finance shared folder shortcut (S:) is missing in your files window.
- The Finance shared folder shortcut (S:) is there, but it does not open.
- You see a message saying the shared folder location cannot be found.

## What You Can Safely Do
1. Save your open work.
2. Close all open programs.
3. Sign out of your computer.
4. Wait 30 seconds.
5. Sign back in.
6. Open your files window.
7. Check for the Finance shared folder shortcut (S:).
8. Open the Finance shared folder shortcut (S:) and check whether your folders appear.

## If It Still Does Not Work
1. Wait 5 minutes.
2. Sign out and sign in one more time.
3. Check the Finance shared folder shortcut (S:) again.

## Contact the Service Desk If
Contact the Service Desk if the Finance shared folder shortcut (S:) is still missing or still not opening after the steps above.

## Details to Include in Your Ticket
- Your full name
- Your computer name (for example, DESKTOP-FB041), if you know it
- Your office location
- Time you first noticed the issue
- What you see now: "Finance shortcut S: missing" or "Finance shortcut S: present but not opening"
- Exact message shown on screen (if any)
- Whether sign out/sign in was tried once or twice
- A picture of the error message, if available

Thank you. The support team will use these details to restore access as quickly as possible.
