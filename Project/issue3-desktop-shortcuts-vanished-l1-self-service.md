# L1 Help Article: Missing App Icons on Your Screen

v 1.0, 14/08/2026, status : Draft

If app icons are missing from your screen, try these safe steps:

1. Log out of your computer and log in again once.
2. Wait 2 minutes after logging in so your screen can finish loading.
3. Restart your computer once.
4. Check your screen again for the missing app icons.

Your files and company data are safe. This issue affects app icons only, not your saved documents.

Please include these details in your help request:
- Your full name
- Team name and location
- Computer name (if visible)
- Time you first noticed the issue
- Names of missing app icons (for example Outlook, Teams)
- Whether they came back after logging out or restart
- A picture of your screen, if possible

Contact the service desk if app icons are still missing after these steps, or if the same issue returns tomorrow.
