# L1 Help Article: Logging In Is Slow or Not Working

v 1.0, 14/08/2026, status : Draft

If logging in is slow or does not work, try these safe steps:

1. Restart your laptop once.
2. Wait 2 minutes before entering your password.
3. Log in and wait up to 5 minutes for your screen to fully open.
4. If it still does not open, log out and log in one more time.

Your files and company data are safe. This issue affects login speed, not your saved documents.

Contact the service desk if you still cannot log in, or if login takes more than 5 minutes after trying the steps above.

Please include these details in your support request:
- Your full name
- Your team and office floor/location
- Computer name (if you can see it)
- Time the issue happened
- What you saw on screen (exact message, or a photo)
- Whether login failed completely or was only slow
