# Partner Update by Lunch — Three Service Issues

## What happened
This morning we confirmed three separate service issues:

1. Some people could not sign in, or sign-in took much longer than normal.
2. One legal user saw Copilot return client matter content they believed they should not have seen.
3. Some people found desktop shortcuts missing.

Impact:
- Work start was delayed for some teams.
- Legal risk review was triggered for the Copilot case.
- Service desk volume increased.

## What is being done now
1. Sign-in issue: we reduced the rollout scope linked to the affected devices to lower login pressure.
2. Copilot issue: we opened a high-priority security review, preserved records, and are checking exactly how access was possible.
3. Shortcut issue: we restored missing shortcuts and stopped the behavior that removed them.

Current position:
- Initial recovery is visible on sign-in and shortcut issues where fixes were applied.
- No issue is being closed yet; all are still under active verification.

## What is still open
1. Sign-in issue: we are confirming stability through the next business-start window.
2. Copilot issue: we are still confirming the exact access path and whether this is permissions-related or a platform defect.
3. Shortcut issue: we are confirming shortcuts stay in place after sign-out, reboot, and next login.

## What partners should expect next
- Next written update: end of day.
- We will share final cause, confirmed fix status, and any follow-up controls before closure.
- If new user impact appears, we will send an earlier update immediately.
