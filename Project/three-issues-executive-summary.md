# Executive Summary — Three Active Issues

Three issues were reported. First, some people on Floor 6 could not sign in or had very slow sign-in on Monday morning. Second, one legal user saw Copilot show client content they believed they should not see. Third, some Floor 6 users lost desktop shortcuts. These issues caused delays and extra support requests.

Actions have already been taken. For sign-in delays, the recent app rollout pressure was reduced for affected devices. For the Copilot concern, a high-priority review was opened, records were kept, and access checks were started right away. For missing shortcuts, required shortcuts were restored and the change that caused the problem was corrected. Early results show improvement where fixes were applied, and final checks are still in progress before closure.

What you need to do: if you are not affected, no action is needed. If you still have sign-in delays, missing shortcuts, or see unexpected content, contact the service desk with the time it happened and your device name.
